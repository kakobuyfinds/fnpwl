How to use a LitBuy spreadsheet

A LitBuy spreadsheet organizes candidate products and their source links. Its value comes from recording the exact item and unanswered questions alongside the price.

01

Identify the exact item

Find a product on CAFANS and open its detail page. Record the product ID, original marketplace link, color, size and quantity. Check whether the price covers one piece, a pair or a complete set.

02

Confirm before paying

Review the buying platforms listed for the product. If using LitBuy, check the listing in your own account and confirm its current option price, domestic delivery, purchasing conditions and return terms before payment.

03

Inspect before dispatch

When your order reaches the warehouse, review its inspection photos against the selected option. Resolve missing pieces, visible damage or measurement questions before approving international shipment.

Before you buy

LitBuy spreadsheet questions

What is this LitBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent browsing guide with three real CAFANS product references, searchable categories and a budget calculator. It is a web catalog rather than a hosted Google Sheet, and it does not process orders or payments.

Where do the product buttons take me?

The LitBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons all open https://cafans.com. Browse or search for the named product there, open its detail page, and review the buying-platform options available for that item.

Are the prices live or shipping-inclusive?

No. They are CAFANS homepage USD reference amounts recorded on November 19, 2026. Current prices may differ by option or conversion. Confirm the item price, delivery charges and any other costs before you pay.

Are these warehouse QC photos?

No. These images are product listing photos linked from CAFANS. They do not show the item received for your particular order. Review your own warehouse inspection photos before authorizing dispatch.

How should I check sizing?

Save the exact listing's measurement chart and selected size. Measure a similar garment you own using the same method, then compare relevant dimensions. Ask about missing measurements instead of relying only on a letter size or a generic fit claim.

What if a product link no longer works?

Search the source catalog using the product name or ID. Check the original marketplace listing where available. Mark unavailable entries in your sheet and confirm any replacement is the same option before ordering.

Does a listing prove authenticity or seller reliability?

No. A catalog entry and its photographs do not establish either. Review supporting evidence relevant to the item and seller, and keep unconfirmed claims visible in your notes.
